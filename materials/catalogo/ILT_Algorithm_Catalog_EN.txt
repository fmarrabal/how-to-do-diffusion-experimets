Practical catalogue of Laplace inversion and DOSY analysis
Complete catalogue of the pinned library’s 51 entries, 9 native Prime routes and 12 context families/components. Not an exhaustive list of all literature or 72 distinct algorithms.

Literature context; check implementation

SVD / TSVD / compression [svd_tsvd]
Literature context; check implementation

FISTA / proximal acceleration [fista_general]
Literature context; check implementation

UPEN / 2DUPEN / MUPEN2D [upen_family]
