Modifications: local LoRA adaptation for bilingual NMR diffusion teaching,
rank 16 on q_proj/v_proj, merged and quantized to GGUF Q8_0. The original
course summaries and synthetic worked examples are documented in the
training and evaluation reports. Original production Beatrix/Prime weights
were not modified. This package contains the modified model, not the
unmodified upstream base.

Teaching simulations, original source summaries and automated software
checks do not constitute experimental calibration, chemical identification,
independent expert review or measured student-learning outcomes.

The student tutor runs locally. It has no network client or instrument API,
and does not execute TopSpin operations or modify experimental data.
